# Custom Reports

This section showcases my experience in designing, developing, and customizing business reports to address specific operational and organizational reporting requirements.

The projects demonstrate how business data is transformed into structured reports that provide meaningful insights, improve data visibility, and support operational decision-making. They highlight the development of customized reporting solutions using SAP Business One, SAP HANA, HANA Queries, and reporting tools to deliver information tailored to specific business processes and user requirements.

Projects may include custom Crystal Reports, SAP Business One layouts, HANA Queries, parameterized reports, formatted documents, operational reports, and customized reporting solutions designed to enhance business visibility and reporting capabilities.

**Areas of Focus:**

- **Crystal Reports Development:** Designing and developing customized reports using database fields, formulas, parameters, grouping, and report formatting.
- **HANA Query Integration:** Developing and incorporating SAP HANA Queries as data sources for customized reporting solutions.
- **SAP Business One Report Customization:** Creating and modifying reports and layouts to meet specific business requirements.
- **Custom Document Layouts:** Developing customized document formats for business transactions, including sales orders, delivery documents, purchasing documents, and inventory-related forms.
- **Barcode & Label Reporting:** Designing customized labels that incorporate item numbers, descriptions, quantities, serial numbers, and barcode functionality.
- **Parameterized Reporting:** Developing reports with user-defined parameters and selection criteria to support flexible data retrieval.
- **Data Presentation & Formatting:** Organizing business information through structured layouts, calculated fields, summaries, grouping, and conditional formatting.
- **Reporting Requirements Analysis:** Translating stakeholder reporting needs into technical specifications and customized reporting solutions.
- **Report Validation & Testing:** Verifying report accuracy, data consistency, calculations, and formatting to ensure reporting requirements are satisfied.

These projects reflect my approach to custom reporting: combining business process knowledge, database querying, and report development capabilities to deliver tailored reporting solutions that improve operational visibility and support organizational decision-making.
