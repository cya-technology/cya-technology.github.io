**Crystal Reports Inventory Label**

Developed a custom Crystal Reports inventory label solution integrated with SAP Business One delivery documents. Configured quantity-based label generation to print individual product labels containing item identification, unique serial numbers, barcodes, and print dates.

**1. SAP Business One – Delivery Window**
Shows item ITM-1001 with a delivery quantity of 10. The quantity determines how many labels are generated.

**2. Crystal Reports – Label Design**
Shows the report fields:
- Item Code
- Item Name
- Serial Number
- Print Date
- Barcode
  
**3. Crystal Reports – Label Output**
Shows individual labels generated from the delivery quantity, each with its own serial number and barcode.

This type of report/label were printed in Zebra printers and used to label shipped inventory.
