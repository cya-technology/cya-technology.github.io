# Data Analytics

This section showcases my experience in data analytics, statistical analysis, and business intelligence, with an emphasis on transforming raw data into meaningful insights that support business performance evaluation and data-driven decision-making.

The projects demonstrate how analytical techniques and technology tools are used to collect, clean, transform, explore, and interpret datasets to identify trends, uncover relationships, and generate actionable insights. They highlight the application of SQL, Python, statistical methods, and data visualization tools to investigate business problems, evaluate performance, and support analytical requirements across different industries and business environments.

Projects may include exploratory data analysis (EDA), data cleaning and transformation, statistical analysis, trend analysis, correlation analysis, regression modeling, performance evaluation, and analytical dashboards.

**Areas of Focus:**

- **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA):** Examining datasets to understand data distributions, identify patterns, detect anomalies, and uncover relationships between variables.
- **Data Cleaning & Transformation:** Preparing raw datasets by addressing missing values, inconsistencies, duplicates, and formatting issues to improve data quality and analytical reliability.
- **Statistical Analysis:** Applying descriptive and inferential statistical techniques to summarize datasets, evaluate relationships, and interpret analytical findings.
- **Trend & Pattern Analysis:** Identifying historical trends, seasonal variations, and recurring patterns to better understand business and operational behavior.
- **Correlation Analysis:** Evaluating relationships between variables to identify potential associations and understand factors that may influence business outcomes.
- **Regression Analysis:** Applying statistical regression techniques to investigate relationships between dependent and independent variables and evaluate predictive relationships.
- **Data Visualization:** Presenting analytical findings through charts, graphs, and interactive dashboards using tools such as Power BI and Python.
- **Performance Measurement:** Evaluating analytical results using statistical metrics and performance indicators to assess model accuracy and analytical effectiveness.
- **Business Intelligence:** Translating analytical findings into meaningful reports and insights that support operational planning and organizational decision-making.
- **Python & SQL Analytics:** Leveraging Python libraries and SQL queries to manipulate, analyze, and investigate structured datasets.

These projects reflect my approach to data analytics: combining technical capabilities, statistical methods, and business understanding to transform complex datasets into meaningful insights that help organizations identify opportunities, evaluate performance, and make informed decisions.
