
# Data Modeling

This section showcases my experience and knowledge in data modeling, database design, and data architecture, with an emphasis on structuring business data to support enterprise applications, reporting, and analytical solutions.

The projects demonstrate how business requirements are translated into structured data models that define entities, attributes, relationships, and data dependencies. They highlight the importance of designing organized and scalable data structures that promote data integrity, consistency, accessibility, and efficient information retrieval.

Projects may include Entity Relationship Diagrams (ERDs), relational database schemas, dimensional data models, star schemas, database normalization, data dictionaries, and documentation illustrating relationships between business entities and database objects.

**Areas of Focus:**

- **Entity Relationship Modeling:** Identifying business entities, attributes, primary keys, foreign keys, and relationships to represent organizational data structures.
- **Relational Database Design:** Designing database schemas that organize business information into related tables while maintaining data integrity and consistency.
- **Dimensional Modeling:** Developing fact tables and dimension tables to support analytical reporting, business intelligence, and data warehousing.
- **Star Schema Design:** Structuring analytical databases using fact and dimension tables to support efficient querying and reporting.
- **Database Normalization:** Applying normalization principles to reduce data redundancy, improve consistency, and establish appropriate table relationships.
- **Data Dictionaries:** Documenting database tables, columns, data types, definitions, and business rules to improve data understanding and maintainability.
- **Data Relationships & Integrity:** Defining primary keys, foreign keys, constraints, and relationships to maintain data accuracy and consistency.
- **Analytical Data Modeling:** Structuring datasets to support reporting, statistical analysis, business intelligence, and predictive modeling.
- **Database Platform Experience:** Applying data modeling concepts across relational database platforms, including Microsoft SQL Server and SAP HANA.

These projects reflect my approach to data modeling: translating business requirements into structured and meaningful data architectures that support reliable information management, efficient reporting, and data-driven decision-making.
