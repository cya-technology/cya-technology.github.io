
# Operational Reporting

This section showcases my experience in developing operational reporting solutions designed to provide real-time and historical visibility into business activities, monitor operational performance, and support day-to-day decision-making.

The projects demonstrate how enterprise data is transformed into meaningful operational reports that help organizations monitor transactions, track workflow progress, identify exceptions, and evaluate business performance. They highlight the importance of accurate and accessible reporting in supporting daily operations, improving process efficiency, and maintaining operational accountability.

Projects may include transactional reports, daily activity reports, inventory status reports, order fulfillment reports, purchasing activity reports, warehouse performance reports, operational dashboards, and exception-based reporting solutions.

**Areas of Focus:**

- **Transactional Reporting:** Developing reports that provide visibility into daily business transactions, including sales orders, deliveries, purchase orders, goods receipts, and inventory movements.
- **Inventory & Stock Monitoring:** Creating reports to monitor stock availability, inventory levels, item movements, inventory discrepancies, and replenishment requirements.
- **Order Processing & Fulfillment:** Developing reports to track order status, delivery progress, outstanding orders, picking activities, and fulfillment performance.
- **Warehouse Operations:** Providing visibility into warehouse activities, including receiving, bin locations, inventory transfers, picking, packing, and shipping.
- **Purchasing Activity Monitoring:** Tracking purchase orders, vendor transactions, outstanding receipts, and procurement activities.
- **Operational Performance Indicators:** Developing reports and metrics to monitor processing times, transaction volumes, fulfillment rates, and operational efficiency.
- **Exception & Variance Reporting:** Identifying incomplete transactions, inventory discrepancies, overdue activities, and operational exceptions requiring attention.
- **Daily & Periodic Reporting:** Developing recurring reports that support daily operations, management reviews, and ongoing performance monitoring.
- **Operational Dashboards:** Consolidating key operational metrics into visual reports that provide stakeholders with an accessible overview of business activities.

These projects reflect my approach to operational reporting: transforming transactional and operational data into timely, actionable information that improves business visibility, supports process monitoring, and helps organizations maintain efficient day-to-day operations.
