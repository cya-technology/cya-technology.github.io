# Business Process Reporting

This section showcases my experience in developing custom ERP reports and business intelligence solutions, with an emphasis on providing operational visibility, improving data accessibility, and supporting informed business decision-making.

The projects demonstrate the development of customized reports designed to address reporting requirements across multiple business functions. By leveraging SAP Business One, SAP HANA, HANA Queries, and reporting tools, these solutions transform enterprise data into meaningful information that helps organizations monitor performance, evaluate business activities, and make data-driven decisions.

Projects may include custom SQL-based reports, SAP HANA queries, Crystal Reports, operational reports, financial statements, management reports, and analytical reporting solutions designed to support day-to-day business operations.

**Areas of Focus:**

- **Sales Reporting:** Developing custom reports to monitor sales orders, deliveries, invoicing, revenue, sales performance, and customer purchasing activity.
- **Customer Relationship Management (CRM) Reporting:** Creating reports that provide visibility into customer records, sales opportunities, activities, customer interactions, and relationship management.
- **Purchasing & Procurement Reporting:** Developing reports to analyze purchase orders, vendor activity, purchasing history, goods receipts, and procurement performance.
- **Inventory Management Reporting:** Creating reports to monitor inventory levels, stock availability, item movements, valuation, inventory transactions, and replenishment requirements.
- **Warehouse Management Reporting:** Developing reports to support warehouse operations, bin locations, inventory transfers, picking, packing, receiving, and fulfillment activities.
- **Financial Reporting:** Creating customized financial reports to analyze general ledger activity, accounts receivable, accounts payable, revenue, expenses, and financial performance.
- **Custom ERP Reporting:** Translating business reporting requirements into customized queries and reports using SAP HANA, SQL, and reporting tools.
- **Data Analysis & Business Intelligence:** Transforming operational and transactional data into actionable insights that support performance monitoring and management decision-making.

These projects reflect my approach to business process reporting: understanding operational requirements, identifying relevant enterprise data, and developing customized reporting solutions that improve business visibility, support operational efficiency, and enable informed decision-making across the organization.
