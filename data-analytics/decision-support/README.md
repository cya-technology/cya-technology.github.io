# Decision Support

This section showcases my experience and knowledge in decision support, business intelligence, and data-driven analysis, with an emphasis on transforming business information into actionable insights that support strategic planning and operational decision-making.

The projects demonstrate how data, analytical methods, reporting solutions, and performance indicators can be used to evaluate business performance, identify trends, assess operational challenges, and support informed decision-making. They highlight the role of business systems and analytics in providing stakeholders and management with the information necessary to evaluate alternatives, prioritize initiatives, and align decisions with organizational objectives.

Projects may include KPI dashboards, performance scorecards, business intelligence reports, trend analysis, forecasting models, scenario analysis, decision matrices, and analytical documentation designed to support business and management decisions.

**Areas of Focus:**

- **Business Intelligence & Analytics:** Transforming business data into meaningful insights through analytical tools, reporting solutions, and visualization techniques.
- **Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):** Identifying and defining measurable performance indicators to evaluate business processes and organizational objectives.
- **Performance Monitoring:** Developing dashboards and scorecards that provide visibility into operational performance, business trends, and progress toward established goals.
- **Trend & Variance Analysis:** Analyzing historical data, identifying patterns, evaluating performance variations, and recognizing potential areas of concern.
- **Predictive Analytics:** Applying statistical methods and forecasting techniques to identify potential future outcomes and support proactive decision-making.
- **Scenario & What-If Analysis:** Evaluating alternative scenarios and assessing how changes in business conditions, resources, or assumptions may affect outcomes.
- **Decision Matrices & Evaluation Frameworks:** Establishing structured criteria to compare alternatives, evaluate potential solutions, and support business recommendations.
- **Operational & Strategic Analysis:** Using business data to identify improvement opportunities, evaluate resource utilization, and support organizational planning.
- **Data Visualization:** Presenting complex information through dashboards, charts, and visual reports that make insights accessible to business stakeholders.

These projects reflect my approach to decision support: combining business process knowledge, analytical techniques, and technology solutions to transform data into meaningful insights that help organizations make informed, strategic, and operational decisions.
