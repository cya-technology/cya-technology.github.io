
# Business Systems Analysis & Project Documentation

This section showcases my experience in business systems analysis, enterprise application support, business process documentation, and project implementation activities.

My work focuses on understanding business requirements, evaluating operational processes, and translating business needs into functional system requirements and practical technology solutions. I have experience developing project documentation and supporting various stages of the systems lifecycle, from requirements gathering and implementation planning to user acceptance testing, deployment readiness, and ongoing system support.

**Areas of Experience:**

- **Functional Requirements Gathering:** Identifying business needs, documenting functional requirements, and translating stakeholder expectations into system specifications.
- **Business Process Analysis:** Understanding existing workflows, identifying operational challenges, and documenting opportunities for process improvement.
- **Project Planning & Implementation:** Developing project plans, implementation schedules, cutover checklists, and deployment documentation.
- **User Acceptance Testing (UAT):** Preparing testing plans, documenting test scenarios, validating system functionality, and supporting issue resolution.
- **Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs):** Creating procedural documentation for system operations, end-user guidance, troubleshooting, and issue escalation.
- **System Support & Issue Management:** Establishing processes for documenting, tracking, prioritizing, and resolving application issues reported by end users.

The documents and project artifacts included in this section demonstrate my approach to requirements management, project coordination, business process documentation, and supporting the successful adoption and ongoing operation of business applications.
