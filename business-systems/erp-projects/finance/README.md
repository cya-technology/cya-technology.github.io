
# Finance

This section showcases my experience and knowledge of ERP financial management functionality, with an emphasis on supporting accounting operations, financial data management, transaction processing, period-end closing activities, and financial reporting.

The projects demonstrate how enterprise resource planning systems support core financial processes by integrating accounting activities, maintaining accurate financial records, and providing visibility into an organization's financial performance. They highlight the use of ERP functionality to manage financial transactions, maintain General Ledger accuracy, support account reconciliation, execute month-end and year-end closing activities, and generate financial information that assists with operational and management decision-making.

Projects may include General Ledger management, accounts payable, accounts receivable, fixed asset accounting, journal entries, account reconciliation, month-end closing procedures, year-end closing activities, and financial reporting.

**Areas of Focus:**

- **General Ledger Management:** Supporting chart of accounts structures, journal entries, account classifications, and financial transaction management.
- **Accounts Payable (AP):** Supporting vendor transactions, purchase invoices, payment processing, and outstanding payable balances.
- **Accounts Receivable (AR):** Supporting customer invoices, incoming payments, outstanding receivables, and customer account management.
- **Fixed Asset Accounting:** Implementing and maintaining fixed asset records, depreciation configurations, accumulated depreciation, and asset-related General Ledger accounts.
- **Financial Reporting:** Developing and supporting financial reports, including balance sheets, income statements, trial balances, and General Ledger transaction reports.
- **Account Reconciliation:** Validating financial balances between subsidiary ledgers and corresponding General Ledger control accounts.
- **Month-End Closing:** Supporting period-end activities, including account reconciliations, journal entries, accruals, depreciation processing, financial reviews, and validation of period balances.
- **Year-End Closing:** Supporting annual financial closing activities, including final account reconciliations, year-end adjustments, fixed asset reviews, depreciation validation, and preparation of financial records for the new fiscal year.
- **Journal Entry Management:** Supporting the recording, review, and validation of financial transactions and accounting adjustments.
- **Financial Data Management:** Maintaining accurate financial information through data validation, account mapping, and transaction integrity checks.
- **ERP Financial Configuration:** Working with financial system settings, account determination, posting configurations, and accounting-related functionality.
- **Financial Process Improvement:** Identifying opportunities to improve accounting workflows, strengthen financial controls, and increase operational efficiency.

These projects reflect my approach to ERP financial management: combining business process knowledge, accounting functionality, and enterprise system capabilities to support accurate financial information, effective accounting operations, reliable period-end reporting, and consistent financial controls.
