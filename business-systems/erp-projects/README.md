# ERP Projects

This section showcases my experience working with Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) systems, with an emphasis on business process optimization, system configuration, reporting, and operational efficiency.

The projects highlight practical applications of ERP functionality across various business operations, including inventory management, order processing, purchasing, warehouse operations, and financial processes. They also demonstrate the role of business systems analysis in identifying operational requirements, improving workflows, and aligning technology solutions with organizational objectives.

Projects may include system configurations, custom reports, SQL queries, process documentation, workflow enhancements, and integrations designed to improve data accuracy, visibility, and overall business performance.

**Areas of Focus:**
- **ERP Administration & Configuration:** System setup, maintenance, and functional configuration.
- **Business Process Analysis:** Evaluating existing workflows and identifying opportunities for improvement.
- **Reporting & Data Analysis:** Developing SQL queries, custom reports, and operational dashboards.
- **Inventory & Warehouse Management:** Supporting inventory control, order fulfillment, and warehouse processes.
- **Systems Integration:** Understanding data flows and integrations between ERP platforms and external business applications.
- **Operational Efficiency:** Leveraging ERP capabilities to streamline processes and improve business decision-making.

These projects reflect my approach to bridging business operations and technology through practical systems analysis and data-driven solutions.
