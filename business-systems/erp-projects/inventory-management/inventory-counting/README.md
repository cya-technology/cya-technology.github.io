## Inventory Counting & Inventory Adjustments

### Overview

Implemented an inventory counting workflow in SAP Business One to reconcile system-recorded inventory quantities against physical stock counts. The process identifies inventory discrepancies and uses Inventory Posting documents to adjust stock balances, supporting accurate inventory records and warehouse controls.

### Business Process

**1. Create Inventory Counting Document**
- Initiate an Inventory Counting document for a selected warehouse.
- Select inventory items to include in the physical count.
- Capture the system-recorded on-hand quantities.

**2. Record Physical Inventory Counts**
- Enter actual counted quantities for each inventory item.
- Support periodic cycle counts and full physical inventory counts.
- Maintain count records for inventory verification.

**3. Review Inventory Variances**
- Compare system quantities against physically counted quantities.
- Identify inventory shortages and overages.
- Review discrepancies before processing inventory adjustments.

**4. Create Inventory Posting Document**
- Generate an Inventory Posting document based on the completed count.
- Apply quantity adjustments for approved inventory discrepancies.
- Update inventory balances within SAP Business One.

### Business Value
- Improves inventory accuracy and accountability.
- Identifies discrepancies between recorded and physical inventory.
- Establishes a structured inventory control process.
- Maintains transaction history for auditability.
- Supports more reliable inventory planning and warehouse operations.
