
# Functional Requirements

This section showcases my experience in business and systems analysis, with an emphasis on identifying business needs, gathering stakeholder requirements, and translating operational objectives into clearly defined functional requirements.

The projects demonstrate how business requirements are analyzed and documented to establish what a system, application, or technology solution must accomplish. They reflect the relationship between business processes, user expectations, and system functionality, helping ensure that proposed solutions align with organizational goals and operational needs.

Projects may include business requirements documentation, functional specifications, user stories, use cases, process workflows, acceptance criteria, and requirements traceability.

**Areas of Focus:**

- **Requirements Elicitation:** Identifying and gathering business and user requirements through stakeholder discussions, interviews, and analysis.
- **Business Process Analysis:** Evaluating existing processes, identifying inefficiencies, and documenting current-state and future-state workflows.
- **Functional Specifications:** Translating business needs into detailed functional requirements that define expected system behavior.
- **User Stories & Use Cases:** Documenting user interactions, system functionality, and expected outcomes.
- **Acceptance Criteria:** Establishing measurable conditions to validate that system functionality meets business requirements.
- **Requirements Traceability:** Maintaining alignment between business objectives, functional requirements, and proposed solutions.
- **Stakeholder Collaboration:** Bridging communication between business stakeholders, technical teams, and solution providers.

These projects reflect my approach to business systems analysis: understanding the underlying business need, defining the required functionality, and ensuring technology solutions deliver measurable business value.
