
# User Acceptance Testing (UAT)

This section showcases my experience and knowledge in User Acceptance Testing (UAT), software validation, and quality assurance, with an emphasis on ensuring that business applications and technology solutions meet defined business requirements and operational expectations.

The projects demonstrate how system functionality is evaluated through structured testing scenarios designed to validate business processes, user workflows, and expected system outcomes. They highlight the importance of developing test cases, executing test scenarios, documenting results, identifying defects, and collaborating with stakeholders to ensure that solutions are ready for production deployment.

Projects may include UAT test plans, test scenarios, test cases, test scripts, test execution reports, defect logs, requirements traceability matrices, and user acceptance sign-off documentation.

**Areas of Focus:**

- **UAT Planning:** Establishing testing objectives, defining scope, identifying stakeholders, and determining acceptance criteria.
- **Test Case Development:** Creating structured test cases based on functional requirements, business rules, and expected system behavior.
- **Test Scenario Design:** Developing realistic business scenarios that represent day-to-day operational activities and user interactions.
- **Test Execution:** Performing functional testing to validate system workflows, business processes, data accuracy, and expected outcomes.
- **Defect Identification & Tracking:** Documenting system defects, assigning severity and priority, and tracking issues through resolution.
- **Requirements Validation:** Verifying that implemented functionality aligns with documented business and functional requirements.
- **Regression Testing:** Revalidating previously tested functionality following system changes, defect corrections, or enhancements.
- **Stakeholder Collaboration:** Working with business users, project teams, and technical resources to review test results and resolve identified issues.
- **Acceptance & Sign-Off:** Evaluating testing outcomes and documenting stakeholder approval before production deployment.

These projects reflect my approach to user acceptance testing: validating technology solutions from the business user's perspective, ensuring that functional requirements are satisfied, and helping organizations deploy reliable solutions that meet operational needs.
