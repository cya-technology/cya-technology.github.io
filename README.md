Welcome to my professional portfolio. This collection showcases my experience, technical capabilities, and project work across business systems analysis, enterprise application support, data analytics, business intelligence, and cybersecurity.

My professional background combines business process analysis with hands-on technical experience in enterprise systems, database development, reporting solutions, and technology operations. I enjoy working at the intersection of business and technology, understanding organizational needs, identifying opportunities for improvement, and developing solutions that support operational efficiency, informed decision-making, and business objectives.

Throughout my experience, I have worked with business applications and database technologies, gathering functional requirements, documenting business processes, supporting system implementations, developing SQL queries, creating custom reports, and building database objects to support business reporting and analytics. These experiences have broadened my understanding of technology infrastructure, information security, and the importance of protecting business systems and organizational data.

Business Reporting: Custom report development using SQL and Crystal Reports, including financial, sales, inventory, warehouse, and operational reporting solutions.

Cybersecurity & Information Security: Defensive security challenges, ethical hacking, firewall administration, networking and routing, Linux application development, and digital forensic investigations.

My goal is to continuously expand my technical knowledge while developing practical solutions that bridge business requirements and technology capabilities.
